# Scope Risk Assessment: Roles Command

## Task

Assess the SCOPE risk of the proposal in `proposals/07-roles-command.md`.
Write your assessment to `risk/07-scope.md`.

## Context

Read these files:
- `proposals/07-roles-command.md` — the proposal to assess
- `research/07-roles-command-needs.md` — synthesis of needs
- `discord_cli.py` — current implementation

## What to Assess

1. **Scope creep**: Does the proposal add anything beyond what was
   requested? Are there unnecessary features?
2. **Missing scope**: Does the proposal miss anything that was requested?
3. **Boundary clarity**: Is the anti-scope well-defined?
4. **Dependency on future work**: Does this proposal create obligations
   for future changes?
