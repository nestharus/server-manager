# Risk Assessment: Scope Risk — Factory Module

## Task
Assess the SCOPE RISK of the proposal in `proposals/21-factory.md`.

## What to evaluate
1. Does the proposal stay within its stated scope?
2. Is it appropriately minimal for the scaffold stage?
3. Does it create future coupling or constraints?
4. Is the anti-scope reasonable and complete?

## Inputs
- `proposals/21-factory.md` — the proposal
- `research/21-factory.md` — research findings
- `research/21-factory-needs.md` — synthesized needs
- `AGENTS.md` — project rules

## Deliverable
Write `risk/21-scope.md` with risk level (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH), numbered findings, and recommendations.
