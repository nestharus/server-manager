# Risk Assessment: Shortcut Risk — Factory Module

## Task
Assess the SHORTCUT RISK of the proposal in `proposals/21-factory.md`.

## What to evaluate
1. Does the proposal take shortcuts that could cause problems?
2. Does it skip validation or error handling that should exist?
3. Does it defer decisions that should be made now?
4. Does it create patterns that will be hard to change?

## Inputs
- `proposals/21-factory.md` — the proposal
- `research/21-factory.md` — research findings
- `research/21-factory-needs.md` — synthesized needs
- `AGENTS.md` — project rules

## Deliverable
Write `risk/21-shortcut.md` with risk level (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH), numbered findings, and recommendations.
