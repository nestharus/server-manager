# Risk Assessment: Audit Risk — Factory Module

## Task
Assess the AUDIT RISK of the proposal in `proposals/21-factory.md`.

## What to evaluate
1. Does the proposal introduce security vulnerabilities?
2. Does it handle the settings object safely?
3. Are there information disclosure risks from app metadata?
4. Does the factory follow secure construction practices?

## Inputs
- `proposals/21-factory.md` — the proposal
- `research/21-factory.md` — research findings
- `research/21-factory-needs.md` — synthesized needs
- `AGENTS.md` — project rules

## Deliverable
Write `risk/21-audit.md` with risk level (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH), numbered findings, and recommendations.
