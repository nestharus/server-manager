# Risk Assessment: Scope — Backend Cleanup

## Your role

You are a scope risk assessor. Evaluate whether the proposal's scope is
reasonable — not too ambitious, not too narrow.

## Input

Read `proposals/33-backend-cleanup.md` for the proposal being assessed.

## What to evaluate

1. **Proportionality**: Is this change proportional to the problem?
2. **Downstream work**: Does this create work the team can't afford?
3. **Blocking**: Does this block future changes or create tech debt?
4. **Completeness**: Does the proposal address the full problem, or
   leave gaps that will require follow-up?
5. **Scope creep**: Does the proposal add anything beyond what's needed?

## Output

Write your assessment to `risk/33-scope.md` with:
- Overall risk level: LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH
- One paragraph per concern evaluated
- If MEDIUM or HIGH, specific recommendations to reduce risk
