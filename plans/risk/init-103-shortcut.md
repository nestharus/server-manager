# Shortcut Risk — INIT-103

## Verdict
LOW

## Summary

Shortcut risk is LOW. The proposal closes the load-bearing shortcut surfaces:
secret-zero is script-driven and idempotent, Helm remains render-time-only,
charts/images/tools are concrete pins, committed raw manifests are checked
against a fresh render, the SOPS extension is one named bootstrap file, the
local proof uses a real k3d reconciliation harness, and the target Kubernetes
Secret value is verified after Operator sync.

The remaining broad choices are explicit, named deferrals that do not weaken
INIT-103's done-state: scoped Operator RBAC belongs to INIT-116, backup/restore
and PVC snapshots belong to INIT-112, credential lifecycle belongs to INIT-115,
and storage `InfisicalSecret` CRs belong to INIT-104.
