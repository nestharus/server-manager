# Shortcut Risk — INIT-102

## Verdict
LOW

## Summary

Shortcut risk is LOW. The current proposal does not satisfy local GitOps with a
dry-run, render-only check, vague future work, or old INIT-009 compatibility
material. It binds INIT-102 to a real local k3d reconciliation path: Flux
controllers are installed from a committed v2.8.6 export, an in-cluster
GitRepository is seeded from a local `gitops/` snapshot, the harness waits on
real Flux Ready conditions with bounded timeouts, and both a sentinel ConfigMap
and SOPS-decrypted Secret must appear in the cluster.

The proposal also closes the previously risky edges without weakening the
done-state. Snapshot drift is checked through both Flux artifact revision and a
cluster-applied snapshot marker. The git daemon uses a PVC and restart recovery
is in the harness. Bootstrap-only SOPS is enforced by an allowlist lint, not only
review convention. Age key rotation is explicitly out of INIT-102 and assigned
to INIT-115, while INIT-102 still owns the idempotent local delivery path needed
for its sentinel proof.

## Convergence statement

INIT-102 converges to LOW shortcut risk. The proposal's done-state is a real
local k3d Flux reconciliation, not a simulation; snapshot drift, git-daemon
restart recovery, post-install idempotency, SOPS decryption, and bootstrap-secret
boundaries are all checked by concrete harness assertions. Deferrals are named
and non-blocking for INIT-102: runtime Infisical belongs to INIT-103, and age key
rotation/custody/break-glass belongs to INIT-115. No MEDIUM or HIGH shortcut
finding remains.
