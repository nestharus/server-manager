## Verdict Rationale

The document clears every hard ban and passes every executive-roadmap-specific check. Hook-to-hypothesis tension holds, problems are felt through operator-level specifics, evidence density is high with citations tied to `market research.md`, `problem.md`, `proposal.md`, and the three executive risk assessments. Borders carry explicit bridges. Risk framing includes trigger indicators, matching the research requirement that executives see risk as understood, bounded, and managed rather than avoided. The Approval Package is concrete and names what stays flexible.

Two soft observations, neither sufficient to fail:

1. **Ending resonance is indirect.** Closure is a feature for this piece type and the document is right to end decided, but a short closing sentence that echoes the "window is open, window is narrow" opening would tighten the frame. This is a polish note, not a rubric failure.
2. **The strategic-outcome sentence (line 12) carries the highest jargon density in the piece** and is the softest candidate for the impress-sentence cut. It survives because a whitepaper's strategic-outcome banner has to compress seven subsystems plus the unifying property into one line. Worth a second look on a future revision, not a re-run.

No rubric stages need to re-run. The document is approved for release as a decision memo.
