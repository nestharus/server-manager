## Non-negotiable Re-scan

Clean.

- **Banned sentence openings (But, And, So, Or, Still, Yet, However, Therefore):** none at sentence start.
- **Banned modal hedges (might, could, perhaps):** none. One soft hedge — "essentially absent" on line 42 — is adverbial rather than modal and is not in the banned list; flagged for awareness, not as a violation.
- **Banned filler (it's worth noting that, the question becomes, one of the few ways, something like):** none.
- **Banned posturing frames (Everyone gets this wrong / People do not realize / The real truth is):** none.
- **Prose triads:** none in the structural sense banned by the skill (Do X, do Y, do Z / How X, how Y, how Z / Every A, every B, every C). Multi-item enumerations in the deck are either bulleted lists, 4+ item lists, or 5-item lists, none of which trigger the triad rule. - **Three-consecutive-same-opening cadence:** none. The Monday-morning scene has two consecutive "You" openers and then breaks pattern. Problem-axis bullets all open with distinct words.
- **[BRACKETED: …] placeholders:** present as expected (founder name, background lines, raise target). Per the style brief these are not failures — the deck is honest about what remains to be filled in.

## Document-type Checks

- **Standard investor spine (problem, solution, why now, market, competition, model, traction, team, ask):** Pass. All nine present and in the expected order, with "Traction" folded into the Business Model section under "**Current Stage — Pre-product.**" That substitution is appropriate for a pre-revenue raise and the research explicitly supports it ("If revenue is not live yet, the deck should present monetization as a staged model rather than a fully proven business").
- **Bottom-up market sizing:** Pass. TAM anchored to `19M weekly active servers × 1 paid bot × $5-$10/mo` with public pricing disclosures. SAM anchored to `190k-570k addressable guilds × $240-$480/year` with the Server Insights 500-member threshold as the guild-share basis. SOM anchored to `~2,000 paying guilds × blended price` at 0.47% of SAM. Every layer has an explicit basis column, not a top-down "fraction of all Discord" framing. The deck also includes the explicit research warning against top-down sizing.
- **Competition framed against substitutes and incumbents:** Pass. Four-row comparative table: Manual status quo, Generic Discord bots, Point tools, Potential incumbent feature overlap. "No competition" framing is absent. The four-item integration moat argument is buyer-relevant (shared causal chain, declared-intent control plane, scoped authority, lifecycle-first objects) rather than logo-chart.
- **Risks explicitly named with mitigations and milestones:** Pass. Five risks, each with four fixed sub-fields (scenario, why it matters, mitigation, containment milestone). The structure matches the research requirement to "pair each with a mitigation plan and a milestone that demonstrates progress." The parallel opening structure across all five risks is a rationed pattern under normal rules, but the style brief for pitch decks makes the investor-legible risk format a higher-order requirement, and the content inside each block varies materially, so the repetition is earning its space rather than templating.
- **Concrete ask (placeholder acknowledgment allowed):** Pass. Ask is $300k-$500k pre-seed range with an explicit bracketed midpoint for the founder to set. Structure is specified (SAFE $7.5M-$10M post-money cap, priced-round alternative $4M-$8M pre-money). Use of funds is allocated by month bucket (1-12, 13-24, 25-36) with MRR-gated hire triggers rather than calendar-gated spending. Runway stated as 24-36 months.
- **Team honest about placeholder status:** Pass. Solo-founder status stated twice (Business Model and Team). Founder background is explicitly `[BRACKETED: ...]` with the in-line instruction "Fill in with actual history; do not fabricate." No fake credentials; no team members invented.
- **Pre-product status honestly acknowledged:** Pass. "**Pre-product.** No revenue. No paying users." in Business Model, and "Humility is currently a solo-founder project" in Team. Traction proxies (strategic-artifact production, reference-community operation) are presented as proxies rather than as paying-customer traction.

## Verdict Rationale

PASS. The deck clears every hard ban and every final-check item. It satisfies all seven pitch-deck-specific requirements in the style brief, including the ones the research calls out as the most common credibility killers (top-down TAM, no-competitor framing, hidden risk, unstructured ask, faked traction). Critically, the deck's honesty about pre-product status, solo-founder capacity, and hybrid market-cap outcome is not a weakness to hide — it is framed as credibility-building, which matches the First Round and Norwest guidance cited in the research.

Two notes for the author, neither a failure:

1. The parallel "The scenario. / Why it matters. / Mitigation. / Milestone." block across five risks is heavy parallel structure. It is justified by the pitch-deck format, but if a reader starts skimming by the fourth risk, consider compressing Risk 5 or letting its substructure vary.
2. "Essentially absent across the field" on line 42 is the one adverbial soft hedge in the deck. Not a violation of the banned-hedging list, but if the market-research claim is strong (which the deck treats it as elsewhere), the word is optional and can go.

No rubric stage needs to re-run.
