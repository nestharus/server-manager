## Sample Fixes

### Instance 1 — Hook (D1)

**Before (line 5):**
`**Strategic outcome.** Deliver a Discord-native guild management platform that treats identity/trust, intake, case management…`

**After (line 5):**
`**The moment.** The competitive window is open because incumbent vendors are exiting, and it is narrow because the harm communities experience in the interim compounds with delay.`

**Technique:** Promoted tension content from the middle of the section to the opening, introducing a hook sentence that frames the two concrete-anchor bullets that immediately follow. "Strategic outcome" was moved below the reframe (line 11) so it now reads as the answer to the reframe, not as the opening claim.

### Instance 2 — Reframe (D2)

**Before (line 11 bullet):**
`- **Dependency order is structurally fixed.** The scoring analysis (see Appendix) finds that dependency-driven phasing is sound and ordering is stable under alternative market readings (…).`

**After (line 9):**
`**Value ordering reveals what to build first.** The problems above are structural rather than tactical, so the right response is not a feature wishlist but an ordered sequence derived from where value compounds across subsystems. The scoring analysis (see Appendix) finds that dependency-driven phasing is sound and ordering is stable under alternative market readings (…).`

**Technique:** Lifted the dependency-stability claim out of a mid-bullet position and made it the payload of an explicit reframe sentence that appears immediately after the hook evidence. The reframe label matches the piece-type adaptation in `WRITING_SKILL_MASTER.md` ("value ordering reveals what to build first"). Citation preserved verbatim. The connecting clause "so the right response is not a feature wishlist but an ordered sequence" supplies the because-link the rubric requires.

## Known Remaining Issues

- The hook sentence joins two `because`-clauses with "and". A strict reader could flag this as a parallel-clause pattern. It is two items, not a triad, and each clause carries a distinct causal claim (why the window is *open*, why it is *narrow*). Acceptable under the "join two items with and and drop the third" fix pattern in the banned-list-prose section.
- The phrase "Vendor instability is actively pushing communities to look for new platforms" now appears once (at the end of the first bullet, line 7) and echoes the hook's "incumbent vendors are exiting" framing. This is restatement-as-evidence rather than duplication: the hook asserts the pressure, the bullet supplies the citation chain. Reviewers who want strict de-duplication could trim the closing sentence of bullet 1.
- "The moment." as a label is a deliberate choice over "The strategic opportunity." — it is shorter and creates urgency. A reviewer preferring explicit "opportunity" framing per the piece-type adaptation could relabel without changing the surrounding text.
